RESEARCH NOTE / 001 INDIVIDUAL COMPANY

Suzlon Energy

Five-year financial statement analysis

Company fundamentals

A structured review of Suzlon Energy's financial performance across a five-year period, focusing on financial statement trends and the relationship between profitability, capital structure and cash generation.

Financial statements Ratio analysis Trend analysis
Open GitHub ↗

Purpose

Understand the company's financial trajectory by examining changes across multiple reporting periods rather than relying on a single year's results.

Analytical process

  • Review income statement, balance sheet and cash flow statement trends.
  • Use financial ratios to investigate profitability, liquidity and leverage.
  • Compare changes across years and identify areas that merit further investigation.

Outcome to communicate

Present the evidence-supported findings, explain the financial implications and disclose limitations. Specific conclusions should match the verified figures and calculations in the repository.

Historical financial performance does not establish future performance or determine an investment recommendation on its own.

RESEARCH NOTE / 002 SECTOR COMPARISON

FMCG financial comparison

HUL · Britannia · Nestlé India

Comparative financial analysis

A comparative study of three major FMCG businesses, examining how financial statement analysis and ratio comparisons can help reveal differences in business performance and capital efficiency.

Peer benchmarking Profitability Cash flow
Open GitHub ↗

Purpose

Compare three businesses operating in the FMCG sector and investigate how their financial characteristics differ over time.

Analytical process

  • Compile comparable financial statement information for the selected period.
  • Calculate and interpret profitability, liquidity, leverage and efficiency ratios.
  • Compare trends while considering company size, business mix and reporting definitions.

Outcome to communicate

Identify meaningful differences between the companies and distinguish observed results from possible explanations. Final numerical claims must agree with the underlying workbook and source reports.

Research limitation

Ratio comparisons can be misleading if periods, accounting definitions or calculations are inconsistent.

05 / Research philosophy

From raw numbers to reasoned conclusions.

A financial conclusion is only as useful as the evidence behind it. My approach is to work from reported information, check the calculations, interpret the trends and remain clear about what the data cannot establish.

A REPEATABLE FRAMEWORK
01

Source the evidence

Identify annual reports, financial statements and relevant supporting disclosures. Record the reporting period and source for each important figure.

02

Standardise the data

Check units, periods, consolidated versus standalone reporting, and calculation definitions before making comparisons.

03

Analyse the financials

Calculate trends and relevant ratios. Read the income statement, balance sheet and cash flow statement together rather than in isolation.

04

Interpret the drivers

Investigate what may explain the results. Separate documented facts from hypotheses that need more evidence.

05

Communicate with context

Summarise the findings clearly, explain limitations and make sure each conclusion can be traced back to its supporting evidence.
